1. Noun (বিশেষ্য)

🧒 শিশু বোঝার অংশ

সহজ ভাষায়: Noun মানে হলো নাম। যেকোনো মানুষ, জন্তু, জিনিস বা বোঝার নামকে Noun বলে।

উদাহরণ:
• রিমার একটি বিড়াল আছে। (Cat = Noun)
• ঢাকা বাংলাদেশের রাজধানী। (Dhaka = Noun)
• প্রেম একটি সুন্দর অনুভূতি। (Love = Noun)

🎓 বিশ্ববিদ্যালয় স্তর

Definition: A noun is a word that functions as the name of a specific object, set of objects, place, or abstract concept. In linguistics, nouns belong to the major lexical categories and can combine with determiners and modifiers.

Noun এর প্রকারভেদ (Types of Noun)

1. Proper Noun (নির্দিষ্ট বিশেষ্য): নির্দিষ্ট ব্যক্তি, স্থান বা বস্তুর নাম।
• Bangladesh, Dhaka, Ibrahim, Monday, January
• সবসময় বড় হাতের অক্ষর দিয়ে শুরু হয়।
2. Common Noun (সাধারণ বিশেষ্য): একই ধরনের সকল বস্তুর সাধারণ নাম।
• boy, girl, country, city, day, month
• Proper Noun-এর opposite।
3. Collective Noun (সমষ্টিবাচক বিশেষ্য): একগোষ্ঠী বস্তুকে একটি নামে ডাকা হয়।
• team, family, crowd, flock, herd, committee, army, audience, bunch, group
• The team has won the match. (singular verb)
• The team have gone to their homes. (plural verb - British)
4. Abstract Noun (ব্যাপ্তিবাচক বিশেষ্য): চোখে দেখা যায় না, শুধু অনুভব করা যায়।
• love, hate, freedom, justice, happiness, courage, wisdom, knowledge
• childhood, friendship, darkness, brightness
5. Material/Concrete Noun (বস্তুবাচক বিশেষ্য): হাতে ধরা যায় এমন পদার্থ।
• water, gold, iron, wood, rice, milk, air
• সাধারণত uncountable।
6. Compound Noun (যৌগিক বিশেষ্য): দুই বা ততো শব্দ মিলে একটি নতুন অর্থ তৈরি করে।
• sunshine, mother-in-law, pickpocket, ice cream, editor-in-chief
• Plural: sunshines, mothers-in-law, pickpockets

2. Pronoun (সর্বনাম)

🧒 শিশু বোঝার অংশ

সহজ ভাষায়: Pronoun হলো এমন শব্দ যা Noun-এর জায়গায় ব্যবহৃত হয়।

উদাহরণ:
• আমি পড়তে ভালোবাসি। (I = Pronoun)
• সে খুব ভালো ছেলে। (He = Pronoun)
• এটি আমার বই। (It = Pronoun)

🎓 বিশ্ববিদ্যালয় স্তর

Definition: A pronoun is a word used in place of a noun. Pronouns help avoid repetition and make sentences smoother.

Pronoun এর প্রকারভেদ

1. Personal Pronoun:
PersonSubjectObjectPossessive AdjPossessive PronounReflexive
1stImemyminemyself
2ndyouyouyouryoursyourself
3rd (m)hehimhishishimself
3rd (f)sheherherhersherself
3rd (n)itititsitsitself
Pluralwe/theyus/themour/theirours/theirsourselves/themselves
2. Demonstrative Pronoun (নির্দেশক):
• this, that, these, those
• This is my book. / These are my books.
3. Interrogative Pronoun (প্রশ্নবোধক):
• who, whom, whose, which, what
• Who are you? / What is this?
4. Relative Pronoun (সম্পর্কবোধক):
• who, whom, whose, which, that
• The man who came here is my uncle.
• The book which I bought is interesting.
5. Indefinite Pronoun (অনির্দিষ্ট):
• some, any, no, every + body/one/thing
• all, each, every, both, few, many, several, some, any, none, nobody, everything, something, anything, nothing
6. Reciprocal Pronoun (পারস্পরিক):
• each other (দুজনের জন্য), one another (বেশি জনের জন্য)
• They love each other.
7. Distributive Pronoun (বিতরণমূলক):
• each, either, neither
• Each of the students has a book.

Common Errors

❌ I vs Me:
• Wrong: Me and him went there.
• Correct: He and I went there.

❌ Who vs Whom:
• Wrong: Who did you meet?
• Correct: Whom did you meet?

❌ Its vs It's:
• Its = possessive (its color)
• It's = it is (it's beautiful)

📋 BCS প্রস্তুতি

BCS-তে প্রায়ই আসে: Who vs Whom, Its vs It's, One of those who + singular/plural verb

Q2. "The book ___ cover is red..." — সঠিক pronoun?

Q5. "Each of the boys ___ present." — সঠিক?

3. Adjective (বিশেষণ)

🧒 শিশু বোঝার অংশ

সহজ ভাষায়: Adjective হলো Noun-এর বৈশিষ্ট্য বা গুণ বোঝায়।

উদাহরণ:
• সুন্দর মেয়ে (Beautiful = Adjective)
• বড় ঘর (Big = Adjective)
• আমি ভালো পড়ি। (Good = Adjective)

🎓 বিশ্ববিদ্যালয় স্তর

Adjective এর প্রকারভেদ

1. Descriptive (বর্ণনামূলক): good, beautiful, tall, red, hard
2. Quantitative (পরিমাণবাচক): some, much, many, little, enough, half, all
3. Numeral (সংখ্যাবাচক): one, two, first, second, double
4. Demonstrative (নির্দেশক): this, that, these, those, such, same
5. Interrogative (প্রশ্নবোধক): what, which, whose
6. Possessive (সম্বন্ধবাচক): my, your, his, her, its, our, their
7. Proper (নির্দিষ্ট): Bangladeshi, Asian, Sunday

Degrees of Comparison (তুলনামূলক)

Opinion → Size → Age → Shape → Colour → Origin → Material → Purpose

4. Verb (ক্রিয়া)

সহজ ভাষায়: Verb হলো কোনো কাজ বা অবস্থা বোঝায়।
• আমি পড়ি। (read = Verb)
• সে খায়। (eats = Verb)
• তারা ছাদে আছে। (is = Verb)

Verb এর প্রকার

1. Transitive: Object লাগে — I read a book.
2. Intransitive: Object লাগে না — He sleeps.
3. Linking: Subject-কে describe করে — She is beautiful.
4. Auxiliary: Main verb-কে সাহায্য করে — is, am, are, was, were, have, has, had, do, does, did
5. Modal: Possibility/ability express — can, could, may, might, will, would, shall, should, must, need, ought to

Regular vs Irregular Verbs

Regular: verb + ed → played, walked, talked
Irregular (তালিকা):
go-went-gone, come-came-come, take-took-taken, give-gave-given, eat-ate-eaten, drink-drank-drunk, write-wrote-written, read-read-read, see-saw-seen, know-knew-known, think-thought-thought, buy-bought-bought, bring-brought-brought, catch-caught-caught, teach-taught-taught, keep-kept-kept, sleep-slept-slept, run-ran-run, swim-swam-swum, sing-sang-sung, begin-began-begun, drink-drank-drunk, ring-rang-rung, swim-swam-swum, drive-drove-driven, ride-rode-ridden, speak-spoke-spoken, break-broke-broken, choose-chose-chosen, freeze-froze-frozen, steal-stole-stolen, wake-woke-woken, fly-flew-flown, grow-grew-grown, know-knew-known, throw-threw-thrown, draw-drew-drawn, show-showed-shown, wear-wore-worn, tear-tore-torn, bear-bore-born, blow-blew-blown

Finite vs Non-Finite

Finite: Tense/person change হয় — He plays football.
Non-Finite (3 প্রকার):
• Infinitive: to + verb — I want to go.
• Gerund: verb + ing (noun) — Swimming is good.
• Participle: verb + ing/ed (adjective) — The running water. / The tired man.

Q1. "He ___ to school every day." — সঠিক?
Q2. "She has ___ the letter." — past participle?
Q3. "Swimming is good exercise." — "Swimming" কোন type?
IELTS Tip: Modal verbs দিয়ে speculation express করুন — "This could lead to...", "It may result in..."

5. Adverb (ক্রিয়া বিশেষণ)

সহজ ভাষায়: Adverb Verb, Adjective বা অন্য Adverb-কে বর্ণনা করে।
• সে দ্রুত দৌড়ায়। (quickly = Adverb)
• সে খুব সুন্দর। (very = Adverb)

Adverb এর প্রকার

1. Manner (কিভাবে): quickly, slowly, carefully, well, badly, hard
2. Place (কোথায়): here, there, everywhere, nowhere, outside
3. Time (কখন): now, then, today, yesterday, already, yet, soon
4. Frequency (কতবার): always, usually, often, sometimes, rarely, seldom, never, hardly
5. Degree (কতটুকু): very, much, quite, rather, almost, too, enough
6. Interrogative: how, when, where, why
7. Relative: where, when, why
8. Conjunctive: however, therefore, moreover, furthermore, nevertheless

Adjective vs Adverb — Common Errors

❌ She sings beautiful. → ✅ She sings beautifully.
❌ He runs quick. → ✅ He runs quickly.
❌ This test is easier than that. → ✅ (correct - adjective after linking verb)
Q1. "She speaks English ___." — সঠিক adverb?
Q2. "He runs ___ fast ___ I can't catch him." — সঠিক?

7. Conjunction (সমুচ্চয় অব্যয়)

সহজ ভাষায়: Conjunction দুটি শব্দ বা বাক্যকে যুক্ত করে।
• আমি পড়ি এবং খেলি। (and = Conjunction)
• সে এসেছে কিন্তু সে থাকেনি। (but = Conjunction)

Conjunction এর প্রকার

1. Coordinating (FANBOYS):
For, And, Nor, But, Or, Yet, So
• I like tea and coffee.
• He is poor but honest.
2. Subordinating:
because, although, though, even though, while, when, if, unless, until, before, after, since, as, so that, whether, whereas, wherever, whenever, however, whatever, whichever, whoever
• Because he was tired, he slept.
• Although it rained, we went out.
3. Correlative (জুটি):
both...and, either...or, neither...nor, not only...but also, whether...or
• Both he and I are students.
• Neither he nor she came.

Common Errors

❌ Although he is rich, but he is not happy. (Although + but = wrong)
✅ Although he is rich, he is not happy.
❌ He is rich but he is not happy, so he is unhappy. (but + so = wrong)
Q1. "He is poor ___ honest." — সঠিক conjunction?
Q2. "___ he was tired, he kept working." — সঠিক?

9. Article (পদবাচক অব্যয়)

সহজ ভাষায়: Article হলো ছোট শব্দ যা Noun-এর আগে বসে।
• a, an, the — এগুলোই Article।
• a = একটি (consonant sound এর আগে)
• an = একটি (vowel sound এর আগে)
• the = নির্দিষ্ট

A vs An — Phonetic Rule

গুরুত্বপূর্ণ: Letter নয়, sound-এর উপর নির্ভর করে।
• a university (উনিভার্সিটি = "y" sound)
• an hour (আওয়ার = "h" নেই)

11. Sentence Types (বাক্যের প্রকার)

সহজ ভাষায়: বাক্য দুই রকম — কোনটা একটা কথা বলে, আবার কোনটা অনেক কথা একসাথে।

By Structure

1. Simple (সরল): একটি independent clause
• He goes to school.
2. Compound (যৌগিক): দুটি independent clause + coordinating conjunction
• He goes to school, and she goes to college.
3. Complex (জটিল): একটি independent + একটি dependent clause
• When it rained, I stayed home.
4. Compound-Complex: দুটি independent + একটি dependent clause
• When it rained, I stayed home, and she went out.

By Function

1. Declarative: He is a good boy.
2. Interrogative: Is he a good boy?
3. Imperative: Close the door. (Command/Request)
4. Exclamatory: What a beautiful flower!

Phrase vs Clause

Phrase: কোনো subject-verb নেই — in the room, a beautiful girl, to go home
Clause: Subject + verb আছে — when I was young, that he is honest

12. Subject-Verb Agreement

25+ Rules:
1. Singular subject → singular verb: He plays.
2. Plural subject → plural verb: They play.
3. Two subjects + and → plural: He and she are friends.
4. Either...or / Neither...nor → verb agrees with nearest subject
5. Each/every + singular verb: Each student has a book.
6. Collective noun → usually singular: The team has won.
7. Uncountable noun → singular: Water is wet.
8. Plural noun + singular meaning → singular: News is good.
9. "One of those who" → plural: One of those who are here.
10. Number of → singular: The number of students is 50.
11. A number of → plural: A number of students are present.
Q1. "The news ___ good." — সঠিক?

13. Voice — Active & Passive

Active Voice: কর্তা মূল ভূমিকায় — I write a letter.
Passive Voice: কর্ম মূল ভূমিকায় — A letter is written by me.

Passive Rules for All Tenses

Present PerfectHe has written a letter.A letter has been written by him.
Simple PastHe wrote a letter.A letter was written by him.
Past ContinuousHe was writing a letter.A letter was being written by him.
Past PerfectHe had written a letter.A letter had been written by him.
Simple FutureHe will write a letter.A letter will be written by him.
ModalHe can write a letter.A letter can be written by him.
Q1. "The cake ___ by my mother." — passive?

14. Narration — Direct & Indirect Speech

Direct Speech: সরাসরি উদ্ধৃতি — He said, "I am happy."
Indirect Speech: পরোক্ষ উদ্ধৃতি — He said that he was happy.

Reported Speech Rules

Pronoun Changes:
I → he/she, my → his/her, we → they, our → their

Time/Place Changes:
today → that day, tomorrow → the next day, yesterday → the previous day, ago → before, here → there, this → that, these → those

Tense Changes:
Simple Present → Simple Past
Present Continuous → Past Continuous
Present Perfect → Past Perfect
Simple Past → Past Perfect
will → would, can → could, may → might, shall → should

Reporting Verbs

Statements: said, told, stated, remarked, exclaimed
Questions: asked, inquired, wanted to know
Commands: ordered, commanded, told, advised, suggested, requested, warned, reminded, begged
Q1. He said, "I am busy." → Indirect?

15. Transformation of Sentences

Affirmative ↔ Negative:
• He is a good boy → He is not a bad boy / He is no fool
• All students passed → Not all students failed

Simple ↔ Compound ↔ Complex:
• Being tired, he slept. (Simple) → He was tired, so he slept. (Compound) → Because he was tired, he slept. (Complex)

Assertive ↔ Exclamatory:
• He is very honest → What an honest man he is!

Degree Change:
• He is the tallest boy → No other boy is as tall as he → He is taller than any other boy

16. Conditionals (শর্তবোধক)

সহজ ভাষায়: "যদি... তাহলে..." — এটাই Conditional।
• Zero: If you heat water, it boils. (সত্য)
• First: If it rains, I will stay home. (সম্ভাবনা)

All 5 Conditionals

TypeCondition (If)ResultMeaning
ZeroIf + present, presentIf you heat water, it boils.সর্বদা সত্য
FirstIf + present, will + V1If it rains, I will stay.সম্ভাব্য
SecondIf + past, would + V1If I were rich, I would travel.অসম্ভাব্য
ThirdIf + had + V3, would have + V3If I had studied, I would have passed.অতীত অসম্ভাব্য
MixedIf + had + V3, would + V1If I had studied, I would be a doctor.মিশ্রিত

Inverted Conditionals (without "if")

• If I were you → Were I you, I would accept.
• If he had known → Had he known, he would have come.
• If it should rain → Should it rain, we would cancel.

Q2. "Had I known, I ___ helped you." — 3rd conditional?
