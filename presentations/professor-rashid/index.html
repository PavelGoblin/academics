01Biography

Dr. Mohammad Mamunur Rashid is a Professor at the School of Science and Technology, Bangladesh Open University (BOU), Gazipur — a position he has held since 24 September 2023. He also serves as the Additional Director (Additional Charge) of the Institutional Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC) at BOU.

His journey at BOU began in 2004 as an Assistant Programmer. He later became a Lecturer in 2010 and progressed through the ranks — Assistant Professor, Associate Professor, and finally Professor. This two-decade trajectory reflects his unwavering commitment to open and distance education in Bangladesh.

Dr. Rashid earned his PhD from Bangladesh University of Professionals (2023), and his MSc and BSc in Computer Science from the University of Madras, India (2003 & 2001). He is a Microsoft Certified Professional and received the prestigious UGC PhD Research Fellowship in 2018.

A multidisciplinary researcher, his work spans technology adoption in healthcare, machine learning, e-learning, and structural equation modeling. He has authored over 15 peer-reviewed papers and several BOU course textbooks, and serves on editorial boards and academic committees.
